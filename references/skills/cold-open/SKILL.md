# Cold Open

Most viewers decide in the first seconds whether to keep watching, often with the sound off.
The opening has to work as a still image and as a question.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Pick the opening image
From the scout photos, the single image that is strangest, most beautiful or most specific to
this game. Not an establishing wide of something generic.

### Step 2 — Make it a question
The image or one short `caption` should make the viewer ask something ("why is the door
taped?"). The answer, if any, comes later.

### Step 3 — Timing
The first shot is 2-4 s (shorter than the genre's average hold), already in motion or with a
detail that rewards the eye. No `black` and no `card` before it.

## Hard rules

- No logo, studio card or rating card at the start.
- No fade in from black on the first shot.
- The first frame alone must read at thumbnail size.
