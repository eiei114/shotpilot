# Demo Teaser

A demo teaser makes one promise: you can play this part today. Show only what the demo contains,
end on the invitation, and keep it short (20-40 s).

## Resource map

- Brief: what the demo covers (`must_show`, `notes`), the event name and the call to action as
  written.
- Stage: the anchors that are inside the demo's area. Skip places the demo does not reach.

## Workflow

### Step 0 — Draw the boundary
List which anchors are in the demo. Shots come only from those.

### Step 1 — Beat map
- hook: the first thing the player sees in the demo, made strange or inviting.
- establish: the demo's space and its main verb.
- escalate: the moment the demo ends on (a cliffhanger or a door), shown but not resolved.
- title: title, then the invitation `card` ("体験版 配信中" only if the brief says so).

### Step 2 — Script
A question or a dare that the demo answers ("この部屋から出られるか？"). The invitation is copied
from the brief.

### Step 3 — Shots
Short: 6-9 shots. End the last shot on the threshold of what the demo leaves unanswered.

### Step 4 — Finish
Transitions `cut`; `dip` before the title.

## Hard rules

- Nothing from outside the demo.
- Event names and "out now" wording only when the brief states them.
