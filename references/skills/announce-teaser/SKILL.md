# Announce Teaser

The viewer has never heard of this game. In under a minute they should know where it takes
place, how it feels, and its name, and want to know more. One idea carried all the way
through beats a tour of features.

## Resource map

- Brief: `core` (the feeling), `must_show`, `pitch`. The plan's one-sentence direction.
- Stage and scout photos: which anchors are strong enough to open and to close on.
- Active design system: grade, letterbox, typeface, card holds.

## Workflow

### Step 0 — Pick the one idea
State, in one line, the premise a stranger can repeat ("a room that has rules"). Every shot
and every card serves it.

### Step 1 — Beat map
`time → viewer takeaway → focal object → transformation → copy/audio → exit`, one row per beat:
- hook (0-4 s): the strangest true image of the world.
- establish: where we are, two or three places, slow.
- escalate: the premise turns; one image that changes the meaning of the earlier ones.
- title: black, then the title, then facts only.

### Step 2 — Script first
Two to four `card` lines carry the premise as a sentence in parts. A `black` of 0.8-1 s before
the title. End with the `title`; add a `message` only when it turns the premise back on the
viewer.

### Step 3 — Shots as evidence
Each shot proves the line before or after it. Open and close on the two strongest anchors from
the scout photos. Keep the last shot before the title the longest.

### Step 4 — Finish
Take grade, letterbox and typeface from the design system. Transitions mostly `cut`; `dip`
before the title.

## Hard rules

- No release date, platform or price unless the brief states it.
- No UI, menus or tutorials on screen.
- One premise; drop shots that start a second one.
