# Title Drop

The title is the payoff of everything before it. It lands hardest after a held image and a
moment of nothing.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — The beat before
End the last shot on its strongest frame and hold it (the longest shot of the last quarter).
Then a `black` of 0.5-1.2 s (the design system's `black` hold), with silence or a cut in the
music.

### Step 2 — The title
A `title` slot, held for the design system's `title_hold` (2.5-4 s). Use the game's title
exactly as written in the project. One title only; no subtitle unless the brief has one.

### Step 3 — After the title
Facts only (see `end-card`), or a last `message`, or nothing. Never another gameplay shot after
the title unless it is a single short sting the plan calls for.

## Hard rules

- The title appears once.
- Never change, abbreviate or translate the game's title.
